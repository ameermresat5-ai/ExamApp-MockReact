4. Entity Relationships
==================================================

Teacher User 1 -------- creates -------- many Exams

Student User 1 -------- submits ------- many Submissions

Exam 1 ---------------- contains ------ many Questions

Exam 1 ---------------- has ----------- many Submissions

Submission many ------- belongs to ---- 1 Student User

Submission many ------- belongs to ---- 1 Exam
